# Related literature (three verified studies)

Indexing status below is deliberately conservative: publisher records establish publication and DOI, not Scopus coverage. No Scopus source record was independently verified in this task, so indexing is marked “not verified.”

## 1. LDA as an evaluated phishing classifier

**Abdelhakim Hannousse and Salima Yahiouche**, “Phishing website prediction using base and ensemble classifier techniques with cross-validation,” *Cybersecurity*, vol. 5, article 19, 2022, Springer Nature. DOI: [10.1186/s42400-022-00126-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42400-022-00126-9). [Publisher full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42400-022-00126-9). LDA is explicitly evaluated among six base classifiers; the article reports 92.87% accuracy in its first experiment. It compares base and ensemble approaches across UCI, Kaggle, and merged datasets. Limitations for our comparison include differing split procedures and an article-reported dataset schema that differs from the local file. Scopus indexing: not verified. Relevant as direct LDA phishing evidence, not a benchmark to compare numerically without protocol alignment.

## 2. Survey of phishing website detection

**Abdelhakim Hannousse and Salima Yahiouche**, “Phishing or Not Phishing? A Survey on the Detection of Phishing Websites,” *IEEE Access*, vol. 11, pp. 18499–18519, 2023. DOI: [10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3247135](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3247135). [IEEE Xplore record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10049452/). The survey organizes blacklist, similarity, and machine-learning approaches and reviews features, datasets, strengths, and gaps. This is not an LDA evaluation; it supplies field context and motivates attention to feature freshness and dataset limits. Scopus indexing: not verified here (IEEE Access is a major indexed journal, but this report does not assert a database record).

## 3. URL-based machine-learning detection

**Qasem Abu Al-Haija and Ahmad Al Badawi**, “URL-based Phishing Websites Detection via Machine Learning,” in *2021 International Conference on Data Analytics for Business and Industry (ICDABI)*, IEEE, 2021, pp. 644–649. DOI: [10.1109/ICDABI53623.2021.9655851](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDABI53623.2021.9655851). [IEEE Xplore record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9655851/). The abstract describes URL-address analysis using neural networks and decision trees and reports a best decision-tree accuracy of 97.40% on an almost balanced recent dataset. LDA is not used. Scopus indexing: not verified. Relevant as supporting URL-feature methodology, not evidence for LDA.

The available evidence supports one directly LDA-specific phishing study and two supporting phishing website/URL studies. This is the honest result of the publisher-record search; two of three papers should not be relabeled as LDA work. All source details and the LDA distinction were checked against publisher pages/abstract metadata [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42400-022-00126-9), [IEEE survey](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10049452/), [IEEE URL study](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9655851/).
